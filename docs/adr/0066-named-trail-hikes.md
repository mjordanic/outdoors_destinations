# Named-trail hikes

Most US trails are not hiking relations. They are `highway=path|footway|bridleway` ways that share a `name` and that no route relation lists (#125). Ingest kept only relations, so a US Catalog held a fifth of what OSM maps. The prototype (2026-11-16) counted the contiguous US on the Geofabrik extracts of 2026-11-15: about 52,700 named-trail hikes with the rules below, 47,000 with the stricter filter, against 17,051 hiking relations that give some 12,000–15,000 relation hikes. Of 20 random samples, 12 were good or acceptable. Slovenia, well covered by relations, gained 8.6 %, mostly short pieces and bike lines.

**A named-trail hike is a built DayHike (ADR-0038) of kind `trail`**, from the source `named_trails` (`dayhike.named_trails`), registered last in `BUILT_HIKE_SOURCES`.

- **Trail ways.** A named `highway=path|footway|bridleway` in no hiking relation, open to walkers (not `foot=no|private`; not `access=no|private` unless `foot=yes|designated|permissive`), with no `footway=*` sub-tag (sidewalk, crossing, ...), not `indoor`, not `golf=*`, not `tunnel=building_passage`. No tracks.
- **Pieces.** Trail ways of one name (case ignored) join where they share a node, or where two of their ends lie within 50 m (`GAP_JOIN_M`, the gap a main walk joins). Ways of the same name that a relation lists are not joined in: that made walks of up to 77 km that repeat relation trails.
- **Walk.** Each piece is walked as a relation's member ways are (`assemble_main_walk`, ADR-0012), so loops, stems and gaps need no new code. The walk starts at the end within 100 m of a `highway=trailhead` node when only one end has one, else by the height rule (ADR-0048), else at the end nearer a trailhead node or parking. Kept when at least 1 km (`MIN_TRAIL_LENGTH_M` since ADR-0068).
- **Not a walkers' trail.** Dropped when more than half its walked metres are paved (`surface` asphalt, concrete, paving stones, ...) or `lit=yes` (a park or city walk); a `snowmobile=designated` corridor not signed for walkers; open to motor vehicles (`motorcycle|atv|ohv|motor_vehicle=yes|designated`); bike-first (`bicycle=designated` or `mtb:scale`, with neither `foot=designated` nor a `sac_scale`); or a ski piste (`piste:type`, same exception). Counted over the whole walk, so a paved bridge does not drop a mountain trail. The motor, bike and piste rules are the stricter filter: 10.8 % of US hikes.
- **Twins.** A walk that is a Twin of a relation DayHike is dropped (the relation hike is listed); of two Twins the one with the lower way id is kept.
- **Label.** The ways' `name`. When another named trail or a relation DayHike has the same name, or the name is a colour or "Trail" only ("Red Trail", "Blue", "Trail": a park's blazes), the nearest place within 5 km of its start is added: "Red Trail near Concord". 27 % of US hikes share a name with another in their region. The NameOrigin is `trail`. (Since ADR-0068 only the colour rule stays here; shared names and "Trail" alone are handled for every hike of the build.)
- **Id.** `built_id(route)` as for any built DayHike: a hike of one way is `start:way.end`, stable until a mapper splits or extends the way or the start flips (the previous starts of ADR-0048 guard the flip).
- **Source.** `osm_source_ref` gives `way/{first way walked}`: a named trail has no relation, and its start node is a weaker record than its way.
- **Page.** The detail of a named trail links the OSM ways its Route walks in place of relations ("Built from OSM ways 302, 301"). Export format unchanged: `built.parts` is empty and `built.route` holds the ways.

**Ingest: the `trails` paths level, and only where asked.** ADR-0065's levels gain `trails` between `graded` and `named`: named `highway=path|footway|bridleway` in no relation, without the tracks that are 60–89 % of what `named` adds, and `highway=trailhead` nodes (`OsmSnapshot.trailheads`; `INGEST_WORK_SCHEMA` 11, so work files are cut again). The source reads only `snapshot.path_ways`, which holds nothing at `graded`. So the paths level is the switch: a US build ingests with `--paths trails` (or `named`), and the Europe build, at the default `graded`, has the same extract filters, the same snapshot and the same DayHikes as before. A build at `named` now also gets named-trail hikes, so ADR-0065's "the same DayHikes at every level" holds for relation, network and stage hikes only.

**Considered Options.** Turning it on for Europe now (Slovenia gains 8.6 %, short pieces, a fifth bike lines, at the cost of a full Europe ingest with more paths; decide after a per-country census of the Europe extract). A minimum of 2 km (would leave 28,945 US hikes and remove most connectors, but also real short trails; 1 km as everywhere). A source switch of its own beside the paths level (a source that has no ways to read builds nothing, so a second switch could only disagree with the first). Reading the level from the region file (ingest takes extracts, not regions; one combined Europe-and-US ingest would need a level per extract, which waits for the worldwide build). An id from the name and lowest way id (would survive a way split, but breaks ADR-0038's one walk, one id). Extending a trail to the nearest parking (#124's composition; a named trail starts where it is mapped to start).

**Consequences.** 65 % of named-trail hikes are one OSM way, their median length is 1.7–2.7 km, and two thirds start more than 300 m from parking or a trailhead node: a quality tier below relation hikes. A trail cut by a road or an unnamed stretch is listed in pieces of the same name. Urban dirt paths (a greenbelt, a power line) pass the filter. A combined worldwide Catalog needs the paths level per extract before the US and Europe can be ingested together.
