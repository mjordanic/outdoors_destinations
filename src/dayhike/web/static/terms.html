GoFindMeAHike

Pick a hike you liked; find the ones most like it, across Europe.

Terms and safety

GoFindMeAHike is a free tool for finding ideas for day hikes. Using it means accepting these terms.

What the data can and cannot tell you

  • The hiking routes come from OpenStreetMap, which is crowd-sourced: a route can be wrong, incomplete, out of date or no longer passable.
  • The measures are estimates: length, elevation gain, steepness, land cover, climate and drive time are computed from map, elevation and climate data, and can be far off. Difficulty grades (SAC) are copied from OpenStreetMap tags as mappers entered them.
  • The site does not show trail conditions, closures, weather, snow, avalanche danger or other mountain hazards.

Stay safe

  • Do not use this site for navigation. Plan with an up-to-date map, check the conditions, closures and weather forecast with local sources before you go, and turn back when in doubt.
  • Choose hikes that suit your fitness, experience and equipment. You are responsible for your own safety.
  • In an emergency, call 112, the European emergency number. In the Croatian mountains, 112 reaches the HGSS (Hrvatska gorska služba spašavanja, the Croatian Mountain Rescue Service); elsewhere, 112 reaches the local rescue services.

You may reuse the data shown here under its licences, with no further restriction from this site:

  • The hike data is a derivative database of OpenStreetMap, offered under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • The elevation profiles and the values derived from elevation (heights, gain, profile shape, steepness, slope aspect) are adapted from FABDEM and shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercially.
  • The other sources and their licences are listed in Data and credits.

The server limits how many requests one client can send per minute, so that a small server stays up for everyone; it is not a limit on what you may do with the data. The code that builds the data is open source under the MIT licence.
