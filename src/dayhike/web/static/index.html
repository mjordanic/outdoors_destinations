    2. What “similar” means

    Weight each measure 0–100; only the ratios matter. Tick off to leave a measure out.

    An estimate: motorway, trunk and primary roads, plus a straight line at 35 km/h to and from them. Good to about ±10 min; a 4 h limit is 4 h ± 30 min.
