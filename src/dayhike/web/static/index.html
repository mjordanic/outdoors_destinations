      About and credits

      A non-commercial demo of the DayHike catalog: OpenStreetMap hiking routes of 1–20 km one way, compared on 27 measures (with Return to start, a hike that does not end at its start is measured with its way back, up to about 40 km). Each measure is calibrated against random pairs of hikes, so weights mean the same on every measure.
